ASCEND Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

ASCEND is a free fitness app with daily quests, XP, streaks and workout plans. This page explains what information the app uses, why, and how you can delete it. If you have a question, write to peakforge.hq@gmail.com.

Who can use ASCEND

ASCEND is for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 13, please do not use the app or create an account.

Information we collect

  • Google sign-in details: when you tap Continue with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the app, and gives us a unique account ID. We use the account ID to keep your progress separate from other people's. The app cannot read your emails, contacts, files or any other Google data.
  • Information you enter: your chosen Hunter name, age, gender, height, weight, fitness goals, experience level, workout days per week, training place (home, dumbbells or gym), and any weight updates you log.
  • Your progress: quests you complete, XP, level, streaks, streak freezes, badges, Shadow Army unlocks, and the workout plan generated for you.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or microphone data. The app does not show ads and does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and show your own progress.
  • To calculate your daily calorie, protein and water targets and build a workout plan for your level and goals.
  • To save your progress so it is still there if you change or reset your phone.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Your account and progress are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) in the Mumbai, India region. Google acts as our service provider and handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy. A copy of your progress is also kept in your browser or app on your device so the app works quickly.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: open Profile and tap Reset all data. This deletes your saved progress and profile from our database and from your device.
  • Delete your account record too: email peakforge.hq@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my ASCEND account". We will delete your sign-in record and any remaining data within 30 days.

You can also remove ASCEND's access from your Google Account settings under Security, "Your connections to third-party apps and services".

Health notice

ASCEND gives general fitness information. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise or nutrition plan, especially if you have a health condition, are pregnant or are returning from an injury. Stop exercising and get help if you feel pain, dizziness or unwell.

Security

Your progress is protected by Google sign-in and by database rules that let each person read and change only their own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your Google account safe with a strong password and 2-Step Verification.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Using the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: peakforge.hq@gmail.com
